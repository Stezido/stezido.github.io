About this service

Terms of use

Hausbot is a personal automation tool, maintained by Stezido for the operator’s own property search.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Personal use

The application is not offered as a public subscription service and has no public account registration. This website explains the application and its data practices. Access to a Gmail account requires that account owner’s authorization through Google.

Property information

Listings and extracted facts can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. A location match is a broad filter and does not establish station walking distance, travel time, property condition, suitability or a recommendation to buy. Check the original listing and relevant documents before making decisions.

Availability

The personal tool is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted service, complete coverage or timely delivery. Its operation depends on Gmail authorization, supported email formats, hosting and Telegram availability.

Third-party services

Google, GitHub, Supabase, Telegram and the listing platforms are separate services. Their own terms apply. Hausbot is not endorsed by or affiliated with those providers. Listing content remains subject to its source’s terms and applicable rights.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy policy for the current data handling practices. Questions can be raised through the site’s issue tracker.
